Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 11, 2026

At Rich Schools, we are committed to protecting and respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains when, why, and how we collect personal information about people who visit our website, contact us, or register to receive newsletters and consultancy services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect and process the following information about you:

  • Personal identification details (Name, email address, phone number) when you book a consultation or submit a contact inquiry.
  • Details about your child's age, current schooling, and educational goals that you choose to provide in our scheduler notes.
  • Your IP address, web browser type, and navigation logs collected through functional site analytics.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal details to:

  • Fulfill, schedule, and confirm your requested consultation meetings.
  • Address, process, and reply to message inquiries submitted through our contact form.
  • Send you newsletter publications, independent school news, and insights (only if you have explicitly registered).

3. Data Security

The security of your personal data is paramount. We implement strict technical and organizational measures to prevent unauthorized access, accidental alteration, or disclosure of details. We never sell, rent, or lease your personal data to third-party brokers.

4. Your Rights

Under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), you hold rights to access, rectify, or erase your personal information. You can withdraw newsletter subscription consents at any time by visiting our Unsubscribe portal or emailing our data controller.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this privacy policy or our data management practices, please contact us at info@rich-schools.com.
