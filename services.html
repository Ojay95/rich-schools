Our services

A complete advisory service for families investing in UK education — practical, personal, and grounded in trusted relationships.

Pillar One

Educational Advice

Navigating the paths of independent education requires forward planning. We provide strategic educational advice that factors in the child's academic profile and your long-term goals.

  • ✔ Guidance on curriculum selection (GCSEs, A-Levels, IB, Pre-U, BTECs)
  • ✔ Formulating university preparation strategies (Oxford, Cambridge, Russell Group)
  • ✔ Structural mapping of study pathways and key exam timelines
Pillar Two

School Placement

We work directly with you to find and match your child to the ideal British day or boarding school. Our deep connection with leading independent institutions enables us to secure placements in schools where children thrive.

  • ✔ Personalized shortlist generation matching your child's learning profile
  • ✔ Application handling, essay reviews, and portfolio compilations
  • ✔ Entrance exam preparation and personalized mock interview practice
  • ✔ Coordinated school tours, family visits, and headmaster introductions
Pillar Three

Pastoral Care & Guardianship

For parents residing overseas, having a trusted, reliable local contact is critical. We provide continuous support, emergency guardianship representation, and act as a caring extension of your family in the UK.

  • ✔ Regular boarding house visits, progress reports, and headmaster dialogue
  • ✔ Emergency point of contact available 24/7 during school terms
  • ✔ Organization of half-term travel, airport transfers, and host family placements
Pillar Four

Academic Assessments

We run independent, honest testing evaluations to map out a child's current standards. This allows families to establish clear timelines, bridge curriculum differences, and select schools with absolute confidence.

  • ✔ Cognitive testing and evaluation of academic baseline skills
  • ✔ Written feedback highlighting strengths and specific improvement areas
  • ✔ Curricular gap analysis for pupils migrating from international school boards
