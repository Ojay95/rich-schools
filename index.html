Who we are

A trusted partner for families navigating UK education.

Rich Schools is a UK-based consultancy nurturing the potential of children from across the world. We've built strong relationships with leading independent schools, sixth-form colleges and tutors throughout England, Scotland and Wales.

Whether you're researching from abroad or planning a move, we offer honest, attentive advice — and we stay close after placement is complete.
