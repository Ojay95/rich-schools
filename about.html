Rich Schools is a UK educational consultancy founded on the belief that every child deserves a school where they can thrive — academically, socially, and personally.

We've spent years cultivating strong relationships with admissions officers, housemasters, head teachers and tutors across the United Kingdom. That network lets us match each family with the right environment, not just the most famous name.

Methodology

How We Work

We take the stress off you through a structured yet personal placement process.

1. Discovery & Evaluation

We begin every relationship with an in-depth conversation — at your home or virtually — to understand your child's academic strengths, character, temperament, and ambitions. We evaluate records and execute preliminary assessments if required.

2. Strategy & Shortlisting

We research and formulate a personalized strategy. We compile a shortlist of boarding or day schools that perfectly match your parameters, introducing you directly to key school contacts and organizing school visits.

3. Applications & Placements

We guide families through the entire application process, assisting with form completions, portfolio compilation, entrance exam preparations, and interview practice. We support you through final offers and registration details.

4. Continued Guardianship & Care

Placement is not the end of our journey. We remain involved to provide support, liaison with housemasters, monitor pastoral care, and offer academic advice throughout your child's school career.
